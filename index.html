From product link to parcel

1. Check the listing and variant

Open the CAFANS product page and locate the original seller link. Check whether OrientDig accepts it. Record the exact size, color and quantity before comparing prices.

2. Review the item at the warehouse

If warehouse QC photos are available, compare the item label and color with your order. For clothing, check a photographed measurement against a garment you already own. Check the return deadline if anything differs.

3. Compare quotes for the same parcel

Use the same destination, packed dimensions and weight for each quote. Check billable weight, insurance, and whether duties or taxes are included before comparing totals.

OrientDig spreadsheet FAQ

What is an OrientDig spreadsheet?

It is a shopping directory you can use to shortlist products, find seller links, and compare variants and reference prices before placing an order through OrientDig.

Why is the checkout price different from the directory price?

The directory shows a snapshot of the item price. Your selected size or color can change the price. Agent fees, shipping, taxes and currency conversion can also affect the amount you pay.

What if the agent cannot use a product link?

Check that you copied the original seller listing from the product details. If the agent still declines the link, check the reason with its support team and choose another listing if needed.
